# Team Charter Template

<!-- Two variants. The accountability row is the switch: outcome (empowered) vs delivery (feature). Never mix labels. Delete the unused variant; never ship placeholders. -->

```markdown
# Team Charter: [Team Name] — Mode: [EMPOWERED | FEATURE]

**Effective:** [YYYY-MM-DD] | **Review cadence:** [e.g. monthly outcome review]

## 1. What We Own
- **Problem (empowered):** [business problem statement: whose pain, how intense, evidence citation]
- **Mandate (feature):** [what is dictated, by whom: contract clause / regulation / signed spec]

## 2. Accountability
- **Outcome metric (empowered):** [metric] from [baseline, anchored] to [target] by [date]. Anchor: [source | ASSUMPTION]
- **Delivery commitments (feature):** [scope + dates + source of obligation]

## 3. Authority
- We decide: [solution choices within constraints; experiment budget; hiring input]
- We do not decide without escalation: [pricing, legal, brand, cross-team APIs — name them]

## 4. Constraints (guardrails)
- Viability: [legal / pricing / brand limits]
- Feasibility: [architecture / platform constraints]
- Compliance: [SOC2 / HIPAA / PCI-DSS as applicable]

## 5. Evidence Access
- [weekly customer-contact cadence; telemetry dashboards; discovery log location]

## 6. Review Question
- Empowered: "Which outcome moved, on what evidence, and what did we learn?"
- Feature: "Did we deliver the mandated scope at the committed date and agreed quality?"
```
