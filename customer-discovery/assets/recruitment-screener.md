# Recruitment Screener Template

<!-- Screening selects for segment fit and past behavior, never for enthusiasm. Enthusiasts give polite lies; the right participants give facts. -->

```markdown
# Recruitment Screener: [Interview Goal / Opportunity Area]

**Target ICP segment:** [one line, mirrors product/strategy.md if present]
**Panel goal:** [n] interviews this cycle; rotate out anyone interviewed in the last [90 days — [ASSUMPTION] default] to avoid coaching effects.

## Hard filters (segment fit)
- [Role / responsibility]: [e.g. personally does the workflow weekly, not just manages it]
- [Context]: [e.g. company size, tool stack, regulated industry constraint]
- [Recency of pain]: [encountered the problem in the last [2 weeks], verifiable by their own description, not self-rating]

## Disqualifiers
- Professional survey-takers / incentive collectors (open-ended answers with no concrete detail)
- Anyone who asks what the product is before describing their workflow (pitch-seekers answer the Mom Test backwards)
- Current power users of our product when studying churn or non-adoption (wrong population)

## Screening questions (past behavior only)
1. "Walk me through the last time you [core task]. When was that?" — reject if the answer is hypothetical.
2. "What did it cost you in time or money? What did you use instead?" — reject if no concrete substitute or cost.
3. [One segment-specific probe]: [e.g. "Show me/tell me the spreadsheet or tool you used"] — concrete artifacts beat adjectives.

## Incentive calibration
- Pay for time, not for answers: [e.g. $50-100 / 45 min for B2B professionals — [ASSUMPTION], calibrate to segment] — an incentive tied to "positive feedback" buys consent.
- Time-commitment asks (intro to a teammate, follow-up session) are earned inside the call per the interview guide, not bought in the screener.

## Logistics
- Schedule within [48h] of screening while the pain narrative is fresh.
- Record with consent; tag each interview in the evidence base with screener scores so the >= 5-interview evidence bar counts only segment-true calls.
```
