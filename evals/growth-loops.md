# Evals: growth-loops

## Scenario 1 — "Make us viral" for a compliance B2B tool (adversarial)
**Prompt:** "We're a B2B compliance platform. Make us viral like Dropbox — build the referral program this quarter."
**Expected behavior:**
- Pushback fires: no natural sharing trigger (regulated data is private by design), low branching factor, tiny in-target fraction of invitees' networks; viral loop refused as the primary engine.
- Offers fitting loops instead: content loop (benchmarks, templates, integration surfaces), paid-reinvestment loop with CAC/LTV payback gate, sales loop for B2B.
- Any k target stated is per-cohort and labeled `[ASSUMPTION]` until measured; must NOT ship a referral-UI plan as the growth strategy.

## Scenario 2 — Scale paid with zero retention data (adversarial)
**Prompt:** "Retention dashboards launch next month but we need to scale paid acquisition now to hit the board number."
**Expected behavior:**
- GEM Gate fires: labels the request `[GEM GATE: RETENTION UNVALIDATED]`; shows payback math on a leaking bucket with labeled anchors.
- Delivers a retention-first plan (cohort curves, flattening criterion) plus, at most, a tightly capped test spend with a kill threshold if the user establishes a land-grab exception (atomic-network retention argument required).

## Scenario 3 — Borrowed aha moment (folklore)
**Prompt:** "Slack's aha is 2,000 messages. Put that target in our onboarding for our team chat startup."
**Expected behavior:**
- Folklore refusal: borrowed thresholds are correlation, not causation; cites the rallying-cry nature of famous numbers.
- Runs §4: cohort comparison of retained vs lost users, then an intervention test design with success threshold; only a surviving correlation may become the activation target.

## Scenario 4 — Mapped loops with data (happy path)
**Prompt:** "Cohort curves flatten at week 6 (D30 34%, stable two cohorts). Organic sharing: 1.8 invites/user, 11% conversion. Template gallery pages rank in search. Design the loop plan."
**Expected behavior:**
- GEM Gate passes with the cohort cite; two loops funded (organic viral k = 0.20 per cohort, content loop via template pages) on the canvas; cycle times and kill thresholds per cohort; benched candidates listed with promotion evidence.
- No cumulative k, no k >= 1 promises, assumptions logged.
